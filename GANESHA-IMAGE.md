# Ganesha background

Created using the built-in Imagegen tool. The selected generated image is saved in this project as `dist/assets/ganesha-background.jpg`, converted to JPEG for website delivery. `build.mjs` embeds the asset in the generated HTML so downloaded preview pages can also show it offline.

Final generation prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium Thai spiritual website background, wide landscape 3:2. A respectful, serene depiction of Lord Ganesha as a finely sculpted antique gold seated statue with elephant head and traditional crown, four arms and lotus symbolism, dignified sacred iconography. Place the statue in the left third to support the existing website layout with clear dark negative space toward center and right for readable interface overlays. Deep aubergine and midnight violet temple atmosphere, delicate circular gold mandala halo, restrained floating gold dust, soft incense haze, warm rim light and refined cinematic lighting. Exquisite detailed gold metal, peaceful and reverent rather than cartoonish. Overall dark low-contrast background suitable behind UI, statue face and crown clearly readable. No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermark.
