# Source boundaries — checked 29 October 2026

## Siamsi

The six interactive cards are website-authored summaries and practical reflections based on the linked Buddhist texts. Their numbers 1–6 are internal identifiers. They are not Wat Sothon fortune slips, predictions, or an official temple publication.

The separate Wat Sothon reference panel summarizes only the example of slip 1 documented on printed page 91 of the 2023 research article by Setthawit Ruengtragul and Mudjalin Laksanawong. That research collected material from an app. It does not authenticate the temple's current 2026 corpus. No missing temple slips have been invented.

## Number ideas

Numbers use Web Crypto with rejection sampling. No prediction model, winning-number data, temple-derived lucky digits, or 80–90% success claim is used. Displayed probabilities are mathematical k/10^n values for matching one uniformly random n-digit result exactly; they are not aggregate odds for every lottery prize category or a measure of near misses.
